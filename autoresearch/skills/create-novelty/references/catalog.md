# Catalog: 100 OSDI/SOSP papers

One paper per row, sorted by year. Selected: Best Paper (every Best Paper of both conferences, 2016–2026), Highly cited (the top 45 of the remaining papers by citation count), Community favorite (the 15 papers most often listed in graduate course reading lists at top universities and in paper blogs).
Archetype names match the 14 archetypes in `novelty-patterns.md`. The full breakdown is in `papers/<id>.md`, and the paper text is in `fulltext/<id>.txt`.

How to use: `grep -i <domain keyword> catalog.md` finds precedents in the same domain; `grep "<archetype name>" catalog.md` (the archetype's English name, e.g. `grep "Skew and repetition" catalog.md`) finds examples of the same kind of insight.
